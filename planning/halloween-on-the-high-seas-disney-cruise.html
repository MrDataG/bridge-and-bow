Halloween on the High Seas: What to Expect on a Disney Cruise

6 MIN READUPDATED OCT 2026BY BRIDGE & BOW

Disney Cruise Line runs a seasonal event called Halloween on the High Seas on select sailings in the fall. Families who want to cruise around Halloween often search for exactly this, so here is what the event is, what is reported to be included, and what to check before you book. Our own September 2026 Disney Destiny sailing was a Halloween on the High Seas sailing.

Check your exact sailing. The event is offered on select sailings, not every cruise in the season, and what is offered varies by ship and itinerary. Confirm that your sailing is labeled Halloween on the High Seas on the Disney Cruise Line website or with your travel agent before you book.

When it sails

DCL Fan, a Disney Cruise Line fan site, describes the event as running on select sailings from September through October. It says the event is back for 2026 but lists no sailing-by-sailing dates, so we are not listing dates here. The reliable source is the Disney Cruise Line sailing calendar, where eligible sailings are marked.

What is reported to be included

These details come from two published write-ups (linked at the bottom) and describe what other cruisers have reported, not a guarantee for your sailing.

  • A Halloween tree in the atrium. Each ship has one, and the Disney Tourist Blog reports a tree-lighting ceremony on the first night.
  • Mickey’s Mouse-querade. A costume party on deck with characters dressed for Halloween, music and dancing. DCL Fan calls it usually the highlight.
  • Character meet-and-greets in Halloween costumes. The Sanderson Sisters meet-and-greet (Minnie, Daisy and Clarabelle) is singled out as a favorite.
  • Trick-or-treating in designated areas of the ship, with candy handed out to guests.
  • Themed treats and drinks at coffee bars, lounges and dining areas.
  • Smaller touches. The Disney Tourist Blog mentions orange and purple floral arrangements, porthole clings, towel animals and a spooky music overlay in hallways and stateroom TVs.

Costumes

Neither source describes a strict costume rule. Costumes are recommended for Mickey’s Mouse-querade and trick-or-treating, and DCL Fan says guests of all ages join in, from full Disney outfits to low-key looks. The Disney Tourist Blog suggests lightweight costumes, because it was warm even with a breeze on its Port Everglades sailing.

How big a deal is it?

Reviewers describe it as extra flavor layered onto a regular Disney cruise rather than a fully immersive Halloween experience. The Disney Tourist Blog’s verdict is that it is the icing on the cake, not the cake itself. Decorations are mostly concentrated in the atrium, some days have little or no Halloween programming, and evening character lines and the atrium were reported as crowded, while afternoon meet-and-greets moved faster.

With a toddler

The same review notes that a toddler loved the characters but was less patient with lines, and that doing meet-and-greets in the afternoon worked better than the evening. It describes Mickey’s Mouse-querade as aimed a bit older than toddlers. Our daughter was three and stayed in the It’s a Small World Nursery, so check nursery hours against the event schedule once aboard.

Our sailing

We sailed the 4-day, 3-night Disney Destiny itinerary out of Port Everglades on September 18–21, 2026, and it was a Halloween on the High Seas sailing. It was also a September full of family celebrations for us, with our anniversary and two birthdays. On the third day there was a trick-or-treat event on the upper deck that kids could take part in. We have a full write-up in our Disney Destiny review and photos in the photo gallery.

Before you book

  • Confirm your sailing is a Halloween on the High Seas sailing.
  • Pick your ship by what you want. Offerings differ by ship, for example a Sanderson Sisters show is reported on the Disney Wish.
  • Pack lightweight costumes. Warm-weather itineraries are common in this season.
  • Check the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app aboard for the event schedule and character times.

Sources: DCL Fan, Your Guide to Disney Cruise Line’s Halloween on the High Seas; Disney Tourist Blog, Halloween on the High Seas review. Neither site is affiliated with Bridge & Bow Travel.
