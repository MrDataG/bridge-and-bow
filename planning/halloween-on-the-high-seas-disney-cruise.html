Halloween on the High Seas: Our Disney Destiny Sailing

3 MIN READUPDATED OCT 2026BY BRIDGE & BOW

Disney Cruise Line offers a seasonal event called Halloween on the High Seas on select fall sailings. Families who want to cruise around Halloween often look for exactly this, so here is what we experienced on our own sailing and how to check a sailing before you book.

Our sailing

We sailed the 4-day, 3-night Disney Destiny itinerary out of Port Everglades on September 18–21, 2026, and it was a Halloween on the High Seas sailing. On the third day there was a trick-or-treat event on the upper deck that kids could take part in. Our daughter stayed in the It’s a Small World Nursery, so check nursery hours against the event schedule once aboard.

We have a full write-up in our Disney Destiny review and photos in the photo gallery.

What we can and can’t tell you

We can only describe what we saw on one sailing. We are not listing event dates, a full schedule or ship-by-ship differences, because we have no firsthand way to confirm them and they change. For those details, use Disney’s own information.

Before you book

  • Confirm your exact sailing is labeled Halloween on the High Seas on the Disney Cruise Line website or with your travel agent. The event is offered on select sailings, not every cruise in the season.
  • Check the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app aboard for the event schedule and character times.
  • If you have a young child, compare nursery hours with event times once you are aboard.
