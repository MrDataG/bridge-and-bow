Cozumel Shore Excursions for Cruise Families

8 MIN READUPDATED OCT 2026BY BRIDGE & BOW

Tours are researched on Viator, not all booked by us. We visited Cozumel in April 2024 on Royal Caribbean’s Voyager of the Seas.

Cozumel is a regular stop on Western Caribbean cruises out of Galveston, Florida and other ports. Most families end up choosing between a beach club day pass, a boat or snorkel tour, or an island tour, and nearly all of them can be booked ahead through independent operators instead of through the ship.

Check your ship’s schedule first. Look up your all-aboard time before you book anything, and leave a buffer for getting back to the pier. Tours listed below vary by date, group size and season.
Family tip: Beach clubs and calm boat tours tend to suit the youngest kids better than ATV or jeep tours, which usually have age and driver requirements. Always confirm age limits on the listing.

What we did in Cozumel

On our April 2024 sailing we spent the day at Playa Mía Grand Beach Park, a beach with a pool and a lunch buffet included. We booked it once we were onboard. Our daughter was 8 months old at the time. If you want a similar day, the beach club passes below list what they include.

What to skip on a port day

Full-day trips to the mainland, such as Tulum, Chichen Itza or the big adventure parks, usually need a ferry and many hours each way. They leave little buffer if your ship sails in the afternoon, so most cruise families are better off staying on the island.
