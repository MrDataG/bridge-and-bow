Nassau Shore Excursions Worth Booking as a Family

7 MIN READNEWBY BRIDGE & BOW

Nassau is one of the most-visited cruise ports in the Caribbean, which means it's also one of the easiest to overbook yourself in. Unlike Castaway Cay or CocoCay, Nassau's excursions are run by independent local operators, not the cruise line itself — so third-party booking actually makes sense here, and often costs less than booking through your ship.

Toddler tip: Skip anything billed as "adventure" or "adrenaline" for the youngest kids — stick to snorkeling tours and beach-club options, which tend to run calmer and more flexible on timing.

What to skip with young kids

ATV and jet ski tours are popular in Nassau but usually have minimum age requirements (often 8+ to ride, sometimes higher to drive) — check the fine print before booking if traveling with a toddler or young child, as these frequently aren't a fit for the family beach-day crowd this guide is written for.
