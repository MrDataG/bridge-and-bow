Castaway Cay: Family Beach vs. Serenity Bay

9 MIN READNEWBY BRIDGE & BOW

Disney's private island splits into distinct zones, and picking the right one for a family with a toddler makes or breaks the day.

Toddler tip: The shaded family beach fills up fast after 10am. Head there first, save Serenity Bay for nap time.

Excursions worth booking

Castaway Cay is Disney's private island — activities here are booked directly through Disney Cruise Line (via the Navigator app or at Guest Services onboard), not through third-party excursion marketplaces.

Castaway Family Beach Cabana
Shaded, close to the splash zone — book early via the Navigator app, these sell out fast
Glass-Bottom Boat Tour
30 min, calm water, good for younger kids — reserve through Disney before boarding
