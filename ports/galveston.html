Galveston Cruise Port Guide: Parking, Pier 10, and Arrival Day

5 MIN READFIRSTHANDBY BRIDGE & BOW

Firsthand — written from our April 2024 Royal Caribbean sailing, with directions from Royal Caribbean's own port instructions.

We sailed Royal Caribbean's Voyager of the Seas out of Galveston, Texas on April 11, 2024, with our 8-month-old daughter. We live in Houston, so we drove to the port, parked, and walked to the terminal. This guide covers what that was like, plus the airport and driving details from Royal Caribbean's port directions for families coming from farther away.

Short version: reserve your parking ahead through the Port of Galveston, bring cash for the porters, and give yourself extra time for security at the pier. The walk from parking was the hardest part of our day with luggage and a stroller.

Where the ship leaves from

Royal Caribbean's port directions list the Galveston terminal as Pier 10 (Texas Cruise Ship Terminal of Galveston Island), at 1402 Harborside Drive, Galveston, Texas 77550. Terminal assignments can change, so check your own booking for the pier before you go. We do not remember which terminal we used, so we are not guessing.

Parking: what we did

We booked our parking ahead on the Port of Galveston's official website, on its cruise parking page. We do not remember the lot or the price, so check current rates and lot options there rather than relying on ours.

What we do remember is the walk. It was a few blocks from where we parked to the terminal, which is manageable on your own and challenging with luggage and a baby in a stroller. Plan for it: pack so you can move your bags in as few trips as possible, and keep one adult's hands free for the stroller.

Royal Caribbean's directions say the Port of Galveston offers pre-paid reserved parking in its official lots with a complimentary shuttle to and from Pier 10. They recommend reserving ahead because spaces are limited in the lots next to the terminal (North Lot, South Lot and Pier 14 Lot). Off-site parking exists too, but it is not affiliated with the cruise line and may not include a shuttle. Ask which applies to your lot before you commit.

Bring cash for the porters

Porters outside the terminal entrance will take your luggage for check-in in exchange for a tip. Have cash on hand. Without it, you carry everything inside yourself, and with a stroller that is a lot to manage.

Security and boarding

Royal Caribbean warns that security procedures at the pier can cause delays, so do not cut your arrival time close. Boarding was easy for us. See our Voyager of the Seas review for our onboard time and check-in notes.

Getting to Galveston from the airport

If you are flying in, Royal Caribbean's directions give these approximate drive times to the pier terminal:

  • William P. Hobby (HOU): about 45 minutes. This is the closer airport, and the one we would pick.
  • Houston Bush Intercontinental (IAH): about 2 hours.

Driving directions from Royal Caribbean

From Hobby: take Beltway 8, Terminal Loop, Airport Blvd and the Gulf Freeway Frontage Road to I-45 South. Take exit 1C toward Harborside Drive/Hwy 275, turn left on Harborside Drive, then left into Royal Caribbean Way to Pier 10.

From Bush Intercontinental: take Beltway 8 East toward US-59, then US-59/I-69 South to I-610 East. Take exit 32A to I-45 South, then exit 1C toward Harborside Drive/Hwy 275. Turn left on Harborside Drive, then left into Royal Caribbean Way to Pier 10.

Routes, terminals and parking rules change. Confirm them with Royal Caribbean and the Port of Galveston before you travel. We did not stay in Galveston before or after the cruise, since home is about an hour away, so we have no hotel or restaurant recommendations to share.
