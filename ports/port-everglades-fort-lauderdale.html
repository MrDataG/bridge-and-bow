Port Everglades with a Toddler: Same-Day Fly-In, Disney Shuttle, and Boarding

8 MIN READFIRSTHANDBY BRIDGE & BOW

Firsthand — written after our September 2026 Disney Cruise Line sailing with a toddler.

We sailed the 4-day, 3-night Disney Destiny itinerary out of Port Everglades in Fort Lauderdale on September 18, 2026 — Castaway Cay and Nassau — with a toddler in tow. We flew in from Houston and boarded the same day. Here is exactly how that went, what the airport transfer did for us, and where Viator tours fit if you would rather arrive early or stay on after the cruise.

Short version: flying in the morning of the cruise worked for us, helped by the cruise line's airport transfer. If you want a buffer — or a day in South Florida — the Everglades is the classic Fort Lauderdale add-on.

Is the cruise line's airport shuttle worth it?

We paid $29 per person each way for our party of three (our daughter included) for the Disney transfer between FLL and the port. For us the benefit was flexibility: because we booked the transfer, we were able to board with no specific arrival time assigned. Guests who are local, or who are not using the transfer, are given a check-in time and have to arrive in that window.

Prices and boarding rules change, so confirm both with Disney Cruise Line before you book — this is what we paid and experienced on our September 2026 sailing.

Flying in the same day: what we would tell a friend

Many cruisers are advised to fly in the day before, as a cushion against flight delays. We went the other way — a 7:45 AM departure that landed at 10:30 AM — and it worked for us. The tradeoff is that there is no slack: a delayed flight puts your sailing at risk, so book the earliest flight you can and keep a plan B in mind. If a buffer matters more to you than a night's hotel cost, arrive the day before.

Arriving early or staying after? Everglades tours on Viator

We did not book a pre-cruise tour (we boarded the same day) and stayed with family in Fort Lauderdale for a few days afterward, so we have not taken these ourselves. They are real, currently listed Viator tours that suit a family with young kids. Confirm price, pickup details, and age rules on Viator before booking.

After the cruise

We stayed in Fort Lauderdale for a few extra days to see family, which made disembarkation day far less rushed. If you can leave a day or two open after your sailing, that is the easiest time to fit in a tour like the ones above — no ship departure to watch.
