Our Story

Bridge & Bow Travel

Bridge & Bow Travel started the way most planning sites should: out of frustration with the ones that already existed. Cruise review sites are either written from press kits and sponsored ship tours, or they're forums buried in a decade of outdated comments. We wanted something in between — real opinions, written after actually sailing, updated when the details change.

We're a Texas family with a toddler, and Bridge & Bow began the way it does for a lot of families: by planning our own first Disney cruise and getting frustrated with what was already out there.

We sailed the Disney Destiny in September 2026, and before that took a Royal Caribbean cruise from Galveston to Cozumel in April 2024. The reviews and port guides here come from those trips, with our own photos. Where a page is research and not something we did ourselves, it says so at the top. We keep adding guides as we sail.

What we do differently

  • Our reviews come from real sailings, and research-based pages say so at the top
  • We flag when something isn't worth booking, not just what is
  • Guides get updated after each trip, not published once and left stale
  • Affiliate links are clearly marked, and we only recommend what we'd book ourselves

How we make money

Bridge & Bow Travel earns a small commission when you book through some links on this site, at no extra cost to you. It's what keeps the site free and ad-banner-free. Full details are in our Privacy & Cookie Policy.
