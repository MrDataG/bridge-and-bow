Disney Destiny 3-Night Cruise Review: What It Is Really Like with a Toddler

9 MIN READFIRSTHANDBY BRIDGE & BOW

Firsthand — written after our September 2026 Disney Cruise Line sailing with a toddler.

We sailed the 4-day, 3-night Disney Destiny round trip out of Port Everglades in September 2026 — Castaway Cay and Nassau — with a toddler who turned three on the trip. September was a month of celebrations for us, with our wedding anniversary, my wife's birthday and our daughter's third birthday all close together, so the cruise doubled as a family celebration. This is a parent's-eye review: what worked for a small kid, and whether three nights is enough.

Verdict: the ship was very clean, the crew was friendly, and there was plenty to do for all ages throughout the day. For a first cruise, three nights gave us a good experience.

Boarding day

After check-in and security at the port, we were announced by our last name as we walked on — a nice welcome. We took photos, went to our room to change, and headed to our first dinner at 1923, then walked a few floors to find things to keep our toddler busy before bedtime. Read the full flight-and-shuttle breakdown in our Port Everglades guide.

The stateroom

We stayed in stateroom 8560, a midship cabin with a verandah. It was very well maintained and the staff were friendly throughout our stay. The stateroom crew even left a towel crab on our bed. There is a laundry facility on Deck 8, the same deck as our stateroom.

Rotational dining: a different themed restaurant each night

Each family is assigned a different themed restaurant on a given night, so we dressed to match. Disney Cruise Line sets your dining rotation and dinner times, and it only shares them once you are aboard, through the cruise line's app. Ours were:

  • 1923 — our first-night dinner.
  • World of Marvel — our second-night dinner.
  • Pride Lands (Lion King) — the third and final night, with a musical-style show during dinner.

Our daughter turned three during the sailing, and the dining staff sang happy birthday to her at every dinner — a nice touch.

Kids' spaces and the nursery

The kids' areas were a highlight. The ones that kept our daughter busy were the Walt Disney Imagineering Lab, the Marvel Super Hero Academy, and the Star Wars Cargo Bay. Because our daughter is three and under and not yet potty trained, she stayed in the It's a Small World Nursery rather than the Oceaneer Club when the two of us took an hour to ourselves, and it was well worth it. Check the cruise line's current age and potty-training rules when you book.

Toy Story splash pad

If your toddler is not potty trained, the Toy Story splash pad is the one to know about: kids who cannot use the other pools can still splash around. It was a great stop on the last day. The kiddie pool, by contrast, is for potty-trained children only.

Characters, photos, and the photo pass

There were plenty of chances to meet Mickey and Minnie and other characters. Lines for photos can get long depending on the character. One night had many characters spread out around the ship, which made it easier to get several photos in one evening. We bought a photo pass, and it covered the crew's photos onboard and at every stop, including on the beach at Castaway Cay.

Seasickness prep

As a precaution, the adults and children used motion-sickness patches and medication, and it helped us a lot. Check with your pediatrician before giving any motion-sickness product to a child.

One pirate night, and fireworks

There was a pirate-themed night with a show and fireworks on the deck. Check the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app for the schedule on your sailing — we did not note which evening it fell on.

The ports

Our itinerary visited Castaway Cay, where we had a beach day, and Nassau, where we chose to stay aboard because we had already visited before.

Is three nights enough?

For a first cruise, yes. We got a real taste of the ship, the dining, and two ports, and the kids were occupied the whole time. If you are comparing ships, see our Disney Fantasy vs. Wish comparison.
