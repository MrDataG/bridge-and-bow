Disney Fantasy vs. Disney Wish: Which Is Better for Families?

12 MIN READUPDATED AUG 2026BY BRIDGE & BOW

Research-based — updated with firsthand details after our September 2026 Disney Cruise Line sailing.

Both ships sail some of Disney Cruise Line's most family-friendly itineraries, but they were built a decade apart and it shows — in the kids' club footprint, the adult-only space, and the overall pace of the ship.

At a glance

CategoryDisney FantasyDisney Wish
Launched20122022
Tonnage~130,000 GT~144,000 GT
Best forLonger 7-night runsShorter intro cruises
Disney Wish: best for families wanting a newer ship and a shorter, lower-commitment introductory cruise.

Disney Fantasy: best for families wanting a longer itinerary and Disney Cruise Line's more established, decade-tested ship experience.

Our take

If this is a first Disney cruise, the Wish's shorter itineraries make sense. If you're sold and want more time at sea, the Fantasy's longer routes are worth it.
