Disney Fantasy vs. Disney Wish: What Families Should Know

7 MIN READUPDATED OCT 2026BY BRIDGE & BOW

Research-based. We have not sailed the Fantasy or the Wish.

What we have and haven’t done. We have not sailed the Disney Fantasy or the Disney Wish. Our own sailing was on the Disney Destiny, a sister ship to the Wish, so we can speak to that ship in our Disney Destiny review. Everything below about the Fantasy and the Wish comes from the sources listed at the bottom of this page, and we have not tested it ourselves.

The Fantasy and the Wish are two of the Disney Cruise Line ships families most often compare. They come from different generations of ships, and that shows in the size, the layout of the kids’ areas and the signature ride.

Where they differ for families

Kids’ areas. TouringPlans reported in 2022 that the Wish has a single Oceaneer Club with five themed areas, including a Marvel Super Hero Academy, Fairytale Hall, an Imagineering Lab, a Star Wars area and a space for the youngest children. It said older ships, which include the Fantasy, split this space into the Oceaneer Club and the Oceaneer Lab. Club age limits and layouts change by ship, so check Disney’s current details. Disney’s Oceaneer Club programming is for children who are potty trained, so it will not cover a baby or a young toddler.

Nursery. Disney offers the “it’s a small world” nursery for children from 6 months to 3 years, for an additional fee and subject to availability, according to a Disney Destiny kids’ club guide. Our daughter stayed in it on our Destiny sailing. We have not checked the nursery details on the Fantasy or the Wish, so confirm ages, hours and fees for your ship.

Dining and ship layout. TouringPlans noted that the Wish replaced the older ships’ Cabanas buffet with a served food hall called Marceline Market, that it has an enclosed Hero Zone for indoor games, and that it has no walking track on Deck 4. Those observations were made on the Wish in 2022, and a ship can change over time.

Itineraries

Disney Cruise Line Blog reported that for Fall 2026 through May 2027 the Fantasy continues 3-, 4- and 5-night Bahamian cruises from Port Canaveral, with stops at Castaway Cay, Nassau and Lookout Cay, plus some 5-night Western Caribbean sailings. We could not confirm the current Wish itineraries or home ports, so check Disney’s sailing schedule. For comparison, our own Destiny sailing was 4 days and 3 nights from Port Everglades.

How we would choose

We can’t tell you one ship is better, because we haven’t sailed either. These are the questions we would ask:

  • Which dates and port fit your trip? Itinerary length and home port often decide it before the ship does.
  • How old is your child? A baby or toddler uses the nursery, not the Oceaneer Club, so the kids’ club layout matters less for the youngest.
  • How much does a newer ship matter to you? The Wish class is newer and larger, while the Fantasy is an older ship with the AquaDuck.
  • What are you comparing against? Our Destiny review shows one family’s experience on a Wish-class ship.
